#Portfolio Website

Overview:
This is a personal portfolio website created to showcase my previous projects and provide potential employers with an overview of who I am, both professionally and personally. The site serves as a digital CV, presenting selected works, technical competencies, and relevant background information.

Purpose:
The primary goal of this website is to:

Present completed projects in a clear and visually appealing way

Offer insight into my professional background, skills, and interests

Serve as a point of contact for professional opportunities

Features
Project Showcase: A collection of my most relevant and recent projects, 
About Me: A section that gives a brief introduction to who I am, my background, and what motivates me.
Contact: A simple and accessible way for visitors to get in touch, either via email or through linked social media profiles.

Responsive Design: Fully responsive layout for seamless viewing across devices.

Technologies Used
HTML5 & CSS3
JavaScript
